HLSI Systems & Technology Ltd

HLSI Systems & Technology Ltd (RC 1422688) is a Nigerian company focused on integrated security systems, surveillance, monitoring and technology solutions for strategic and critical environments.

Our areas of focus include homeland and border security, maritime and seaport security, aviation and airport security, pipeline and facility protection, surveillance, monitoring and systems integration.
